# Progress

## Current state

Milestone 09 is complete. Documents cite works from a local BibTeX file with `[@key]`, grouped keys, page locators, and narrative `@key` citations, in a built-in author-date or numeric style in English or German. A bibliography of the cited works follows at the end, or where `::: bibliography` stands, also in two columns. It is in the table of contents and the outline, and every citation links to its entry. Decisions are in [decisions](DECISIONS.md#2026-10-07-milestone-09-citations-and-bibliography); the syntax, styles, and fields are in [authoring](AUTHORING.md#citations-and-bibliography).

Milestones 11 and 12 are complete and merged. The [business documents amendment](BRIEFING.md#business-documents-amendment) of 2026-10-07 added milestones 11 to 13; release acceptance (10) waits until they are complete. Themes can now use installed fonts (looked up by the CLI only, collection faces included) with weights 100 to 900, tracking, capitals, keep-with-next, and custom styles applied with `{.name}` ([decisions](DECISIONS.md#2026-10-07-milestone-11-installed-fonts-and-custom-styles)). They can set prose narrower than wide blocks, unmirrored margins, title pages and bands made of anchored slot groups, `meta` values, and `{pages}` ([decisions](DECISIONS.md#2026-10-07-milestone-12-page-geometry-covers-and-bands)).

Milestone 13 is complete. A `::: table` directive holds a list table whose cells hold paragraphs and lists, with `{span=n}` cells, `{.name}` on rows and cells, and `align` and `widths` attributes where `*` columns fill the frame. Themes set `top-rule`, `header-rule`, and `row-rule`, and a row style's `rule-below` drops or changes the rule below its row. Title and band slots may name custom styles, and bands apply tracking and capitals. A wide table narrower than the frame starts with the prose instead of centering across the frame ([decisions](DECISIONS.md#2026-10-07-milestone-13-rich-tables), [authoring](AUTHORING.md#list-tables)).

The follow-up fixes of 2026-10-07 closed the open follow-ups from milestones 03 to 13 or settled them as [decisions](DECISIONS.md#2026-10-07-follow-up-fixes).

Placeholders, statistics, and drafts were added on 2026-10-07 at the owner's request, outside the milestone plan. Documents use `{words}`, `{chars}`, `{build-date}`, and the other placeholders that do not depend on the page in their text; slots gain the statistics, `{section-page}`, and `{section-pages}`; `draft: true` sets a LaTeX-style diagonal watermark from the new theme `watermark` section ([decisions](DECISIONS.md#2026-10-07-placeholders-statistics-and-drafts), [authoring](AUTHORING.md#placeholders)).

Book structure was added on 2026-10-09 at the owner's request, outside the milestone plan. `::: front-matter`, `::: main-matter`, and `::: back-matter` start parts with their own page numbers (roman, then arabic, also in PDF page labels); `{-}`, `{.unnumbered}`, and `{.unlisted}` mark headings; `::: toc` places the contents; `***` and `---` are scene breaks. The theme's `chapters` section opens level 1 headings on new or odd pages, sunk, with the number above, an ornament, a drop capital, and a small-caps lead-in; `pages.opening` styles chapter pages. See [decisions](DECISIONS.md#2026-10-09-book-structure), [authoring](AUTHORING.md#books), and [themes](THEMES.md#books). Uncommitted, on branch `book-structure`.

Current milestone: [10: Release acceptance](milestones/10-release-acceptance.md).

Variable font weights and italics were added on 2026-10-07 at the owner's request. File faces can use `{file: ..., variable: true}`; installed variable fonts are detected automatically. The existing weights select `wght` instances, and italics use an `ital` axis or a separate italic file. Shaping and PDF embedding share coordinates, and unavailable block faces remain errors. See [decisions](DECISIONS.md#2026-10-07-variable-font-weights-and-italics) and [themes](THEMES.md#fonts-and-images).

### Merge and typing note

On 2026-11-05 an outside or agent change and the author's unsaved edits in the same sentence became a conflict, see [sentences in a merge](DECISIONS.md#2026-11-05-sentences-in-a-merge). Edits in different sentences of a paragraph still merge, Git's conflicts still never merge, and an agent change stays under review. `cargo test -p druck-desktop` passed 685 with 1 ignored.

Typing time was measured, not guessed, on a temp copy of the book made a project through `adopt::make_project`, with the shipping profile (fat LTO) and `--features measure`, on the M1 Pro with two 60 Hz displays. The gate's typing median was 16.2, 16.6, 15.8, 15.7, and 15.8 ms, p95 30 to 32 ms, at loads of 3.0, 2.8, 2.4, 2.0, and 5.9. The app's part is small:

- **CPU per key is about 3 ms.** `xctrace` Time Profiler over 45 s of the gate's typing: the main thread was busy 1,322 ms, 2.9 percent, and drawing frames took 781 ms of that. `DRUCK_TRACE`: the handler 0.06 ms median, the text's prepaint 0.83 ms, its paint 0.14 ms. Shaping the edited paragraph is most of the prepaint.
- **The rest is waiting.** From the key's handler to the end of paint is 11.2 to 11.6 ms median and 18.7 to 19.7 ms p95 over three runs. Of that, about 10.2 ms median and 17.5 ms p95 come before the text's prepaint: the wait for the display link, half a 16.7 ms frame on average, and about 2 ms of rendering the rest of the window. No key missed a frame.
- **The gate's own timer adds 2.4 to 3.3 ms median and 17 to 18 ms p95.** Its "key timer lateness" is measured from when a key was due to when its task ran. The main thread was idle 97 percent of the time, so this is not app work in the way. It is most likely the system coalescing timers for an app that is not frontmost, which was not verified. A real keystroke does not go through that timer.
- **The contents card was drawn in every frame of the profiled run,** about 200 ms of the 1,322 ms, likely because the pointer rested over the floating window's left edge. That is the environment, not typing.

So there is no real and cheap fix left that would move the median: all of the app's work together is about 3 ms. No code was changed for typing. The owner decided that the gate measures from when the key's handler runs to the end of the first paint that shows it, as `DRUCK_TRACE`'s `input_to_paint_us` does, and reports the timer's lateness apart, judging nothing by it. The gate does so since the [third review](DECISIONS.md#2026-11-05-third-review-findings). Its floor at 60 Hz is half a frame plus the work, and its p95 cannot go below one frame, 16.7 ms. The target was not changed.

## Blockers and follow-ups

- Phase 2: a long-lived process holds about five times the memory of one job on macOS (the allocator keeps freed memory; the program's own heap is flat). Measure it on Linux, and consider a note for service authors or an allocator choice.
- Phase 2: `Overrides::parse`, `Project::open`, and `DocumentText::read` return `Vec<Diagnostic>` rather than `Failure`, so `?` into a `Failure` works but a plain `Box<dyn Error>` needs `map_err`. Unify if an outside client complains.
- Phase 2: only `druck-desktop` draws from a `Layout`, and the core's "no printing, no filesystem" rule has no automated check.
- Desktop: CI now builds `druck-desktop` on macOS (not yet run). The galley uses Georgia until the first successful layout, and takes only the body's font, size, and line height from the theme.
- Typesetting amendment: krilla writes `DisplayDocTitle` only for PDF/UA files; line-end hyphens are hard hyphens in tagged text; margin notes in two-column sections all go to the outer margin, and placement inside a single column is not supported; an unplaced figure can precede a waiting placed one; a split callout's box may touch the footnote area when `footnotes.gap` is smaller than `callouts.padding`; the bibliography heading does not open a chapter like the index heading does; index page numbers use the link color; the default palette's keyword blue and type teal are close.

- Reference matching: title blocks drop leading slot space at the page top, and cover metadata cannot use a different top margin from the body. See [reference decisions](DECISIONS.md#2026-10-07-named-user-themes-and-pdf-reference-themes).
- Unquoted decimals in `meta` and `author` lose trailing zeros (`1.50` reads `1.5`); serde-saphyr keeps source text only for plain string fields.
- A URL without a single slash that is wider than its column is still an error.
- `{pages}` and other page placeholders are rejected in the body; supporting them would need the settling loop to carry the page count.
- Reading speed for `{reading-time}` is fixed per language, and `{build-date}` uses UTC, not the local time zone.
