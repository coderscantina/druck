A tariff for every load

Two columns T

The Golling tariff of 1561 names forty-one kinds of load. A Fuder of salt, the wagon measure of about eight Zentner, paid 6 Kreuzer at the bridge.

A pack horse with two Zentner paid 2. A cart of iron paid 4, a cask of wine 3, and a herd of cattle a Kreuzer for every five head.

Carriers who came back empty paid nothing, which is why the daybook shows so many return loads.
