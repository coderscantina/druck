The Golling tariff of 1561 names forty-one kinds of load. A Fuder of salt, the wagon measure of about eight Zentner, paid 6 Kreuzer at the bridge, and a pack horse with two Zentner paid 2. A cart of iron paid 4, a cask of wine 3.

The clerk at Golling kept his rates on a single board, and the daybook repeats them every spring with only the coins changed. Nobody needed to read to pay.
