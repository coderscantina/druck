Desk overview. Cmd+Escape or a pinch zooms out of the text to the whole manuscript, laid out like the book's contents page: one entry per file in reading order, grouped by part, each with its first line, length and state. Arrows move the focus, a drag reorders, Return zooms back into the entry under focus. At 40 chapters the parts wrap into more rows and the page scrolls, typing a title filters it, and the focused entry stays in view; files outside the book are set aside after the back matter, the loose draft as an entry and research, notes and probes as one list.
