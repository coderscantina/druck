Lens, then unfold. One strip with two depths: at rest it is Lens's compact ticks, one per heading, the section holding the caret in full ink; as the pointer comes near, the ticks under it grow like a dock and only the nearest title surfaces; when the pointer rests on the strip, every tick unfolds in place into its own line of the outline, with the part and the neighbouring chapters above and below, and a click jumps. Round 1 scored Unfold 9 with "Mixture of lens and unfold" and Scroll map 2 for being "too spacious", so this keeps Unfold's outline, adds Lens's approach, and sets both at 8 and 20 px steps.
