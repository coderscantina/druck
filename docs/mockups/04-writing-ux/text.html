5 Tolls and tariffs

Salt is the one tax a man pays at every meal, so he pays it without noticing.Hallein toll book, 1561

The bridge at Golling was rebuilt in stone in 1549, and from the 3rd of March 1561 every load that crossed it was weighed, sealed and written down. The clerk sent a fair copy to the Hofkammer in Salzburg and kept a rough one he called the daybook.

Each wagon was checked in the same order:

  • the seal on every barrel
  • the weight in Zentner, against the pass
  • the toll in Kreuzer, never in salt
