# 19: Publish view

## Goal and prerequisites

The step that turns a manuscript into a finished book, as in [the desktop briefing](../DESKTOP_BRIEFING.md#publish). Requires milestone 18. See P1 and P3 on [the first mockup board](../mockups/01-surfaces.html) and S1 on [the second](../mockups/02-agent-style-history.html).

## Deliverables

1. **Spread and rail.** Engine pages in spreads, and a right rail in the reference form language: book details, style, fonts, images, then proof and export at the foot.
2. **One zoom scale.** Fit, closer, and further out to a contact sheet of every page. A click on the sheet zooms into that spread. Zoom in the status line.
3. **Proof slip.** A click on a line opens the paragraph's text at the page in the theme's font. Pages reflow on a pause, with the slip staying on its paragraph. It offers a page break before and keep together, written as the existing directives. Its foot links to the paragraph's style.
4. **Proof tab.** The book's diagnostics and typesetting warnings grouped by chapter, with a key to walk them and marks on the pages.
5. **Style index.** A searchable index of the theme's 30 sections and one section at a time. Hand-designed: page geometry, body text, headings, fonts, tokens, chapter openings, running heads, and contents. The others open as JSON in the same panel.
6. **Moved here.** Details, fonts, images, the theme picker, and export, with an Export button in the title bar.

## Completion criteria

On a copy of the owner's real book: a word changed in a slip is on the pages within about 1 second, and Write shows the same text. A slip edit that moves a paragraph to another page keeps the slip with it. No text is lost through 100 scripted slip edits. Spread and contact sheet are inspected on screen in both appearances. A style change reflows the pages and writes only the changed field. Directives from the slip appear in the source view.
