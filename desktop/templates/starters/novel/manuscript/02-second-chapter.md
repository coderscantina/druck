# The second chapter

Morning came late on the island. Anna woke to the sound of someone splitting wood below her window and lay still for a while, counting the strokes.

Each file in `manuscript/` is a chapter, in the order `druck.yaml` lists them. Cmd+\ shows the chapters at the left, where a drag puts them in another order and the plus at the foot adds one. At the top of the window, Publish shows the pages, and Write brings you back.
