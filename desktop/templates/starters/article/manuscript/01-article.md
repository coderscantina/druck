## A first section

Start with what the reader should take away. An article is short enough to read in one sitting, so its first paragraph has to earn the second, and its title has to earn the first.

This is sample text. It shows the elements of an article: sections, emphasis, a quotation, a list, and a note. Replace it with your own.

## What an article holds

An article runs in one column from its title to its last line. Sections have no number. *Emphasis* is set in italics and **strong emphasis** in bold, and a link such as [example.com](https://example.com) shows in colour.

> A quotation is set apart from the text around it. Use one when the exact words matter.

A list sets out items of the same kind:

- one point,
- another point,
- and a last one.

A note holds what would interrupt the sentence.[^note] It stands at the foot of the page.
