# The first chapter {#sec:first}

A page is read long before it is understood. Before the first word, the reader has seen the width of the margins, the length of the lines, and the weight of the type, and has decided how much patience the book deserves.

This is sample text. It shows what a chapter of this book looks like: the chapter label above the title, sections, a quotation, a list, a table, a note, and a citation. Replace it with your own first chapter.

## A section

Sections divide a chapter and are listed in the contents under it. They have no number in this book. The running head of a right-hand page names the chapter, and the left-hand page names the book.

> A quotation is set apart from the text around it. Use one when the exact words matter more than a summary.

A claim that needs a source gets a citation. @bringhurst2004 [p. 26] gives 45 to 75 characters as a comfortable line, and this page stays within that.[^measure] The works you cite are listed at the end of the book.

[^measure]: A note holds what would interrupt the sentence. It stands at the foot of its page.

## Another section
