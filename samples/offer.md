# Summary

Thank you for asking us to set the 2027 edition of the Northwind product handbook. This offer covers the conversion of your existing Markdown sources, a theme in your corporate design, and the production of print and screen PDFs in English and German.

The handbook has grown to about 180 pages with 60 tables and 40 figures. Most of the text is already in Markdown, so the work lies in the design of the pages, the tables, and the review of every page before release. We set the prose in a narrow column for comfortable reading and let tables use the full width of the page, so that wide price and specification tables need no smaller type.

# Scope

The work is divided into four parts. Each part ends with a review by your team, and the next part starts once you have approved the previous one. Changes you ask for during a review are included; changes to approved parts are billed by the hour.

- **Theme.** A theme in your typefaces and colours, with the cover, running footers, and table styles shown in your brand guide.
- **Conversion.** Cleaning up the Markdown sources, adding labels for cross-references, and moving figures into the shared asset folder.
- **Production.** Print PDFs with crop marks for your printer and screen PDFs with bookmarks and links, in both languages.
- **Review.** Two rounds of page-by-page review with your editors, and a final check of every cross-reference and page number.
